Educational tool, not financial advice. Funding-rate differentials, fees, slippage, and liquidation distances shown here are estimates derived from public market data and simplified assumptions (see each opportunity's detail panel). Daily/weekly/monthly projections assume the current funding differential holds constant, which real markets rarely do. Fees use standard taker-fee assumptions, not your personal fee tier. Always verify live order-book prices, funding schedules, and account margin requirements directly on the exchange before trading. Cross-exchange funding arbitrage carries execution risk (legs can fill at different times/prices), exchange counterparty risk, transfer/withdrawal risk, and liquidation risk on both legs — a $100 position leaves very little room for adverse price moves once fees and slippage are subtracted.
